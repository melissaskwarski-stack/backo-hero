Who we are

About
BACKO

A back office solutions company built in Colombia, focused on small businesses and growing companies worldwide that deserve better operations without the corporate overhead.

Our Story

BACKO started with a simple observation: businesses worldwide are drowning in back office work that has nothing to do with why they started their business. Answering emails, scheduling calls, chasing leads, managing their website — hours every week stolen from what actually matters.

At the same time, Colombia has an enormous pool of educated, bilingual, motivated professionals who want to build serious careers in the digital economy.

Build the bridge.

BACKO connects businesses worldwide with two things they actually need: AI systems that handle the repetitive work automatically, and real Colombian professionals who handle everything else. Not a staffing agency. Not a software product. Both — under one roof, managed by one team.

We're based in Colombia. We work for businesses around the world. And we believe that where you're from has nothing to do with the quality of work you can deliver.

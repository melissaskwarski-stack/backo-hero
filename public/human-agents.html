How Hiring Works
From assessment to working
with your agent

We handle everything between "I need help" and "they're working for me." No job posts, no interviews, no HR paperwork.

01
Take the BACKO Assessment

Tell us what your business needs, what your biggest challenges are, and what kind of person you're looking for. Takes 2 minutes — free.

02
Discovery call with BACKO

We review your assessment results and dig deeper into what you actually need — tasks, hours, tools, communication style. We scope the role precisely.

03
We recruit your agent

We search our network and external sources for the right person. We screen, interview, and vet candidates so you don't have to.

04
BACKO onboards them

We handle onboarding, tool access, and initial training. We brief them on your business before they ever speak to you.

05
You start working together

Your agent is ready. BACKO stays in the background — available if issues arise, monitoring performance, and adjusting if needed.

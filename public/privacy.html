Legal

Privacy Policy &
Data Protection

Last Updated: April 4, 2026  ·  USA & International

MYBACKO (NIT: 1115070852-6), operating as BACKO, provides high-end AI and Human Agent solutions for businesses worldwide. This policy outlines our commitment to protecting the privacy of our clients, particularly those operating within and connecting with the United States.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide back-office and automation services:

  • Business Contact Data: Name, corporate email address, phone numbers, and professional social media profiles.
  • Operational Data: Information provided during the BACKO Assessment, including industry details and workflow requirements.
  • No Sensitive Data: BACKO does not collect Sensitive Personal Information (as defined by US privacy laws) such as social security numbers, health data, or precise geolocation.

2. US Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA & State Laws)

If you are a business or resident in the United States, you have specific rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Know: You can request a disclosure of the personal information we collect and how it is used.
  • Right to Delete: You may request the deletion of your corporate contact data from our active marketing databases.
  • No Sale of Data: BACKO does not sell, rent, or lease your personal or business information to third parties. We only share data with service providers (like Google Cloud) strictly for the purpose of fulfilling our contract with you.

Important for US Clients: We explicitly do not sell your data. This protects you under California (CCPA), Virginia, and other state-level privacy laws — and protects us from complex regulatory obligations.

3. AI Processing & Data Isolation

  • Strict Isolation: Your business data is stored in isolated environments. We do not use client data to train general AI models or for the benefit of other clients.
  • Third-Party APIs: We utilize secure APIs (e.g., Google Cloud, Anthropic) to process automation. These providers are bound by enterprise-grade security standards and do not have rights to use your data for their own purposes.

Your Trade Secrets Stay Yours. Your data never crosses into another client's environment. We take commercial confidentiality seriously — it is a core part of how we operate.

4. Human Agents & International Transfers

  • Bilingual Talent (Colombia): Our agents operate from Colombia. By using our services, you consent to the cross-border transfer of data necessary for human-led support and back-office tasks.
  • Legal Safeguards: All agents are vetted and sign comprehensive Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). Access to your CRM or corporate email is strictly limited to the scope of the hired service.

5. Data Storage & Security

All information is hosted on Google Cloud Platform (GCP), utilizing industry-standard encryption (AES-256) and security protocols to ensure your data is protected against unauthorized access.
